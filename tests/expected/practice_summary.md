## Limitations (read first)

- Self-timed at a keyboard: typing speed is part of every time, and nobody is watching.
- The math trainer asks weak categories more often (adaptive weights), so the overall accuracy is pulled down relative to an even mix. Compare within a category.
- An answer that arrives after a session's deadline is discarded and not logged.
- Days are UTC days. A category with few answers has a noisy accuracy.
- Market-game P&L is dominated by luck over a few games; the share of quotes that contain the fair value is the steadier number.
